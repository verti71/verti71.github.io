Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. Operator: Verti71 — an independent, non-commercial private project. Contact: email.

PL: Projekt jest prywatny i przeznaczony do użytku właściciela. Poniżej opisano zakres korzystania z danych Google i zasady ich ochrony.

1. Google account permissions and data

With the owner's explicit authorization, the private utilities may access the following Google services:

  • Gmail (read-only): selected messages and their metadata, text and supported attachments needed for the owner's private organizational tasks. The application does not send mail on the owner's behalf.
  • Google Photos (append and edit app-created data): media and related descriptions that the application adds or manages for the owner. The application does not use these permissions to manage unrelated content.

Access is limited in the application's operation to the information needed for the owner's chosen tasks. The permissions granted by Google may be broader than the subset of data the application actually processes.

2. Purpose and use

Google user data is used solely to organize the owner's own information and media in the owner's private workspace. It is not used for targeted advertising, sale, profiling or training general-purpose AI models.

3. Storage and retention

Authorization credentials and a limited record of processed items, their identifiers, descriptions and status may be stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure. Files being processed may be held temporarily, including in a queue awaiting owner action. Media added to Google services is retained there according to the owner's account settings and actions.

Locally stored records, credentials and queued files remain until deleted by the owner. Revoking Google account access stops further authorized Google API use but does not automatically erase previously stored records, files or items already added to Google services.

4. Sharing and service providers

Data is not sold or shared for independent advertising purposes. Processing may involve Google APIs and the hosting provider used to run the private workspace, solely to provide the described functionality. There are no public user accounts.

5. Security

Access to the operational systems and credentials is restricted; communications with Google APIs use encrypted connections. Credentials and personal data are not intentionally published on this website.

6. Revocation, deletion and questions

Access may be revoked through Google Account permissions. Requests or questions about deletion of data stored by the project can be sent to the contact address.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the private project changes. The publication date above will be revised when applicable.
